Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. In short: AppliedIn keeps your data on your computer and has no servers that receive it.

What AppliedIn stores

When you apply for a job, the extension saves details visible on that page — company, job role, location, salary, the job link, and an excerpt of the job description — together with the date, status and any notes you type. This data is stored only in your browser on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or sell your personal data or your job list.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts in the extension.
  • We do not read your passwords, resume, email or messages.

Permissions, in plain words

PermissionWhy it is needed
storage, unlimitedStorageTo keep your applications on your device without a size limit.
alarms, notificationsTo show one daily follow-up summary and interview reminders while your browser is open.
activeTabSo “Use this tab” in the Add form can fill in the current page link.
scriptingOnly used if you turn on “track any company career website”.
Job portal websitesSo the extension can recognise your application on LinkedIn, Naukri, Internshala, Indeed, Glassdoor, Unstop and similar sites, and on common company hiring systems.
Optional: all websitesOff by default. Only if you switch on career-site tracking and approve the browser prompt. Email, banking, social and shopping sites are always excluded.
Optional: Gmail (mail.google.com)Off by default. Only if you switch on “Detect replies from my Gmail” and approve the browser prompt. AppliedIn then reads the sender, subject and preview of emails shown in your open Gmail tab, on your computer, to spot interview invitations, assessments or rejections from companies you applied to. Email content is not uploaded, not saved and not shared — only the resulting status change (and the email subject, to show you why) is kept in your activity log. Turn it off at any time.
appliedin.vercel.appTo download a small public file of page selectors. No data about you is sent in this request.

Your control

Use Back up to download your data, Restore to bring it back, and delete any application at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data from your browser.

Children

AppliedIn is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/MOHITH-KVS/appliedin/issues.
