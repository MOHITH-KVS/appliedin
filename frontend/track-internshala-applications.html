Track your Internshala applications automatically

Applying to 30 internships in a week is normal. Knowing which ones replied is not. AppliedIn keeps a clean list of every Internshala application, free for students.

How AppliedIn tracks your Internshala applications

  1. Open an internship or job on Internshala.
  2. Click Apply now and submit your answers. When Internshala shows that your application was sent, AppliedIn saves it.
  3. Internship listings are tagged as Internship automatically, including the stipend when shown.
  4. Open “My applications” on Internshala and AppliedIn updates statuses like viewed or shortlisted for jobs you already track.
What gets saved for each application
Company, job role, location, salary or stipend when the page shows it, the job link, the date and time, and a copy of the job description so you can read it again before the interview even if the posting is removed.

Built for students

Set a daily goal such as 5 applications, and watch your streak grow. Reminders tell you when an internship has had no reply for a week, so you can send a polite follow-up.
