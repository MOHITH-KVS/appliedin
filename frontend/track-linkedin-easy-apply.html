Track LinkedIn Easy Apply applications automatically

LinkedIn only shows “Applied” on the job itself. There is no simple list you can search, sort or export. AppliedIn builds that list for you as you apply.

How AppliedIn tracks your LinkedIn applications

  1. Browse jobs on LinkedIn normally. AppliedIn reads the company and role from the job details panel.
  2. Click Easy Apply and finish the form. When LinkedIn says your application was sent, AppliedIn saves it instantly with Undo.
  3. For jobs that say Apply and open the company website, AppliedIn asks “Did you apply?” when you return to LinkedIn.
  4. Visit your LinkedIn applied-jobs page and AppliedIn can update statuses such as “viewed” on jobs it already tracks.
What gets saved for each application
Company, job role, location, salary or stipend when the page shows it, the job link, the date and time, and a copy of the job description so you can read it again before the interview even if the posting is removed.

Tracking external applications

Many LinkedIn jobs send you to a company site such as Workday, Greenhouse or Lever. AppliedIn carries the job details to that tab, so if you finish the application there it is saved with the correct company and role.

Never apply twice

If you open a job you already applied to, a small banner shows when you applied and the current status. In search results, tracked jobs get a green badge.

Keep your search organised

Use the Kanban board to move jobs from Applied to Interview and Offer, and the insights page to see which days and portals give you the most replies.

Questions

Does it work with LinkedIn Easy Apply?

Yes. It detects the “application was sent” confirmation and saves the job you applied to.

Will LinkedIn ban me for using this?

AppliedIn does not click, scrape lists of jobs, or send automated applications. It only notes the job you open and apply to yourself.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no server. Your list is stored in your browser on your computer.
