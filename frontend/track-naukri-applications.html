Track your Naukri applications automatically

You apply to dozens of jobs on Naukri every week. A month later you cannot remember which company you applied to, or whether the HR call is for a job you really applied for. AppliedIn fixes that without any typing.

How AppliedIn tracks your Naukri applications

  1. Open any job on Naukri as usual. AppliedIn quietly reads the company and role from the page.
  2. Click Apply. If Naukri sends you to a confirmation page, AppliedIn still remembers which job you were on.
  3. When Naukri shows that you applied, the application is saved and a small message appears with Undo and Edit buttons.
  4. Search results show a green “✓ Applied” badge next to jobs you already applied to — so you never apply twice.
What gets saved for each application
Company, job role, location, salary or stipend when the page shows it, the job link, the date and time, and a copy of the job description so you can read it again before the interview even if the posting is removed.

Why a Naukri-specific tracker helps

Naukri lets you apply with one click, so it is easy to apply to the same role twice or to forget it entirely. Recruiters often call weeks later. With AppliedIn, you open the extension, type the company name and see the role, the date, and the job description you saved.

Add reminders so applications do not go cold

AppliedIn can remind you to follow up after 7 days with no update, and marks applications as “No Response” after 30 days so your list stays honest. You can change both numbers in settings.

Questions

Is the Naukri tracker free?

Yes. AppliedIn is free with no premium plan, no ads and no account.

Does AppliedIn see my Naukri password or resume?

No. It only reads the job title, company and similar details visible on the job page. Everything is stored on your own device.

What if the Naukri page changes?

Portals change their pages sometimes. AppliedIn can download a small public selector file to adapt, and if it cannot read a job with confidence it asks you to confirm instead of saving something wrong.
